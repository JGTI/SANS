## Core Highlights

1. **Zero-Login Public Experience at `/`:** Opens directly into the **3-Tab SANS Biomarker Predictor** in **English (`EN`)** by default, with a 1-click **`EN | ES`** language selector and **Reset Demo** button.
2. **6 Core Ocular Biomarkers:** (1) Mean OCT thickness ($\mu\text{m}$), (2) Eye axial length ($\text{mm}$), (3) Simulated hyperopia ($\text{D}$), (4) Papilledema Frisén scale ($0\text{–}5$), (5) Intraocular pressure ($\text{mmHg}$), and (6) Prior $-6^\circ$ head-down tilt exposure on Earth.
3. **Longitudinal Timeline (Before · During · +30, +60, +90 Days Post-Flight):** Tracks per-eye progression (`OD` / `OS`) and preserves missing visits (such as +60d) without false interpolation.
4. **3 Reference Scenario Models:** One-click switching between **Stable (`CASE-STABLE-01`)**, **Worsening (`CASE-PROG-02`)**, and **Unscanned (`CASE-NOSCAN-03A`)**.
5. **Scientific & Ethical Integrity:** Distinguishes **Ready for review**, **Prioritize review**, and **Missing data** while keeping **Fitness not evaluated** unless signed by an authorized clinician.
