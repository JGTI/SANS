## 3. Scientific Transparency & Ethical Boundary
1. **Deterministic Ophthalmic Calculations:** Spherical Equivalent ($SE$), Snellen $\to$ LogMAR conversion, Thibos power vectors ($M, J_0, J_{45}$), and rule-based longitudinal delta evaluation ($\Delta\text{TRT} \ge +20\ \mu\text{m}$).
2. **Explicit Data Provenance:** Calibrated synthetic demo cases (`FICTICIO_DEMO`), $-6^\circ$ head-down tilt Earth analogs (`ANALOGO_TERRESTRE`), and interpolated simulation frames are explicitly labeled and never misrepresented as real patient records.
3. **Non-Diagnostic Scope:** The platform outputs operational review states (**Ready for review**, **Prioritize review**, **Missing data**) and displays **Fitness not evaluated** unless signed by an authorized clinician.
